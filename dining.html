Dining

Two dining rooms, a cellar bar, and a café that opens before the front desk does.

The Malinowa Room

Dinner, under the original chandeliers

Two connected dining rooms — one seats 150 beneath a restored ballroom ceiling, the other a quieter 50-seat parlour next door. Kitchen leans on Polish staples, revisited without much fuss.

Open daily, 18:00 – 23:00. Smart casual. Reservations recommended on weekends.

Grand Cellar Bar

Wine list built over three decades

Set in the same vaulted cellar as the spa's entrance corridor, the bar keeps a short cocktail list and a long, mostly Central European wine list. Live piano on Friday evenings.

Open daily, 17:00 – 01:00.

Aurelia Café

Coffee and pastry, before the day starts

A street-level café that opens before the front desk does. Regulars order without looking at the board; everyone else is welcome to ask what the almond crescent actually is.

Open daily, 06:30 – 12:00.
