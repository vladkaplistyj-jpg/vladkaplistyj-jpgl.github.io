The Hotel

Built for a city that never stopped moving

Hotel Aurelia opened its doors in 1889, when the boulevard outside was still lined with carriage wheels rather than trams. It has stayed open through every version of the city since — never the newest building, always the steadiest one.

A full restoration in recent years repaired the original plasterwork and stair rail by hand, then quietly added the wiring, insulation, and climate control a modern stay expects. Nothing about that work is meant to be noticed. That's the point.
